\stable{stab:predicates}{\textbf{Ordering a role reversal depends on the predicate rather than on the model.} For each of the eight pairs, the distance ratio $\rho$ and the share of that pair's items on which the role reversal is the closer of the two. The symmetric predicate leaves the name transposition meaning-preserving; the asymmetric one makes the identical transposition a role reversal, therefore a role-sensitive score would put $\rho$ above one on every row. None does. The spread runs in both directions: some pairs are separated decisively and well above the margin, while on others the reversal is the closer of the two in every item. No model is therefore role-sensitive across predicates, and which case a given relation falls into cannot be read off the model.
